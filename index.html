      Brand Introduction

      Pure Coconut Oil, Crafted with Care

      Oils are an essential part of everyday cooking, adding flavour and aroma to the food we enjoy. At Nandhika, we believe the oil your family uses every day should be pure, natural and carefully processed.

      Nandhika Virgin Standard Coconut Oil is produced using selected coconuts, controlled infrared drying and cold pressing at 47°C. Our process is designed to retain the coconut's natural characteristics while delivering clean, consistent and high-quality coconut oil for everyday cooking.
